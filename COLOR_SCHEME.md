# Warm Editorial Color Scheme (Site-Wide)

## Primary Palette
- **Ink**: `#14110f` - Main background
- **Charcoal**: `#1c1713` - Depth layer for gradients
- **Sand**: `#f5efe6` - Primary text and surfaces
- **Clay**: `#caa06a` - Soft highlight
- **Copper**: `#b85c3a` - Primary accent
- **Olive**: `#7b8a5a` - Secondary accent
- **Stone**: `#b9afa3` - Muted text

## Gradient Combinations

### Hero Sections
```
bg-gradient-to-br from-ink via-charcoal to-dark
```

### Section Backgrounds
- Soft panels: `bg-primary/5` and `bg-primary/10`
- Cards: `bg-primary/10` with warm shadows

## Accent Usage
- Primary buttons: `bg-secondary text-dark hover:bg-secondary/90`
- Outline buttons: `border-secondary text-secondary hover:bg-secondary hover:text-dark`
- Links/icons: `text-secondary hover:text-secondary/90`

## Text Colors
- Headings: `text-white` (global display font)
- Body: `text-sand/80`
- Muted: `text-sand/60`

## Why This Works
- **Premium feel** with warm neutrals and copper accents
- **Readable** on dark backgrounds without harsh contrast
- **Consistent** across cards, buttons, and highlights
- **Client-ready**: polished and calm, not neon
